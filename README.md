### About

I build products end to end, mostly in DeFi: trading apps, wallet integrations, indexers, smart contracts, mobile apps and MCP servers for AI agents.
At [Fibrous Finance](https://www.fibrous.finance), a multi-chain DEX aggregator on Starknet, Base, HyperEVM and Monad ($400M+ volume, 880K+ users), I built and maintain the trading app and created FibX and Fiberscope.
